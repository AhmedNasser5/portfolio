About Me

Building AI that matters

I'm an AI & Machine Learning Engineer and Data Analyst based in Egypt, graduating from the Faculty of Computer Science at Benha National University (Major: AI & ML). My journey in AI started with a deep curiosity about how machines learn — and evolved into building real, production-deployed systems.

I specialize in Computer Vision and deep learning, having led the technical development of a mobile AI chatbot that recognizes Egyptian artifacts from photos and returns historical descriptions, now live on both Google Play and the App Store, and tested at the Grand Egyptian Museum.

Beyond AI, I'm equally passionate about Data Analysis and Business Intelligence — turning raw, messy datasets into clean dashboards and actionable insights using Power BI, SQL, and Python. I believe good data storytelling is just as important as good modeling.
